# Antifragile: Things That Gain From Disorder

**Author:** Nassim Nicholas Taleb  
**Published:** November 27, 2012  
**The Big Idea:** Antifragile systems are strengthened by disorder  
**Applying to agentic development:** Have a process for performing retrospectives on agentic processes that underperform or behave unexpectedly. Turn those retrospectives into better processes.
